Our Objectives
What this tool sets out to achieve for the research community
🎯
Simplify Literature Search
Generate precise, database-specific Boolean queries in seconds — no manual trial-and-error.
🔒
Privacy-First Design
Runs on your local machine via Ollama. Your research data never leaves your computer.
🌐
Multi-Database Coverage
Supports the five most widely-used academic databases with correct syntax for each.
👩‍🔬
Accessible to All Researchers
Designed for all experience levels — from undergrads to senior academics — with guided steps.
⚡
Accelerate the SLR Process
Cut hours of title/abstract screening to minutes with AI relevance classification.
🔬
Improve Search Quality
Extract the most meaningful terms to produce high-recall, high-precision search strings.
